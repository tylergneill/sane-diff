- The transcripts don't always set prose apart from verse: a verse quotation
  that follows a prose paragraph is its own block, with the line
  introducing it (e.g. `tatra ... |`) on its own line.
- Introductory bits like `(xyz) āgame`, `(xyz) mūlam` are their own
  paragraph.
- Set verses out as verse even when they lack a closing `||` or a number.
